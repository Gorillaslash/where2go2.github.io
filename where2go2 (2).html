About

where2go2 shows how far you can travel from a starting point within a chosen amount of time, walking, cycling, driving, or by wheelchair.

Map tiles and geocoding by OpenStreetMap contributors and CARTO. Terrain tiles by OpenTopoMap (CC-BY-SA). Satellite imagery, terrain base, and place labels by Esri. Reachable-area calculations by OpenRouteService.
